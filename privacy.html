Tiny Explorer Adventure Game  /  Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: March 2026

🌍 About This Policy

Welcome, explorer! This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Tiny Explorer Adventure Game app and visit this website. We believe your privacy is a treasure worth protecting — just like the hidden gems in our game. We are committed to being clear and straightforward about our practices.

🛡️ Information We Do Not Collect

Tiny Explorer Adventure Game does not collect personal data from its players. Specifically:

  • We do not gather names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.
  • We do not track device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses to identify you.
  • We do not collect location or geolocation information.
  • We do not record or transmit gameplay statistics such as scores, levels reached, or time spent playing.

📦 On-Device Storage

Certain information — such as your game progress, unlocked levels, collected treasures, and preference settings (e.g., sound or display options) — may be saved locally on your device. This data remains entirely on your device and is never sent to us or shared with any third party. Removing the app from your device will delete this locally stored data.

🔗 External Links and Third Parties

Tiny Explorer Adventure Game does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising SDKs that collect your personal information. This website may contain links to external services such as the Apple App Store. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, which are beyond our control. We encourage you to review their policies when navigating away from our site.

👶 Young Explorers

Tiny Explorer Adventure Game is designed to be enjoyed by adventurers of all ages and is rated 4+ on the App Store. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, our practices are consistent with applicable children's privacy regulations. We do not knowingly solicit or store data from children or any other age group.

🔄 Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we do, the "Last updated" date shown above will be revised accordingly. Your continued use of the app or this website following any updates signifies your acceptance of the revised terms. We suggest revisiting this page periodically to stay informed.
